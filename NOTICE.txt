vitaplex_qm.suprx, in this folder, is VitaPlex's quick menu plugin: the song
and working buttons in the Music row of the quick menu, and VitaPlex's music
played on in the system shell. VitaPlex loads it into the system shell and
talks to it through message pipes. It is a program of its own, based on the
quick menu plugin of ElevenMPV-A by GrapheneCt, and licensed under the GNU
General Public License, version 3: see vitaplex_qm.LICENSE.txt, beside it.
VitaPlex itself is licensed under the Apache License, version 2.0.

Its source is in VitaPlex's repository, https://github.com/breezyslasher/vita_plex,
folder src/qm_plugin_psv, with how it is built.

The plugin also carries the head.bin template and the way to make it of
VitaShell, by TheFloW (https://github.com/TheOfficialFloW/VitaShell,
resources/head.bin and package_installer.c's makeHeadBin), licensed under
the GNU General Public License, version 3: plugin/head_bin.h, and the VPK
section of plugin/main.cpp. Its inflater (include/sc_inflate.h) follows
puff, by Mark Adler (zlib's contrib/puff, zlib licence).
